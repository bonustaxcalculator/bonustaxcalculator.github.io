Cookies Policy

Clear explanations outlining how and why our web application processes persistent data tokens.

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

This document details our policy regarding cookies and how they are deployed across Bonus Tax Calculator. To make our digital calculator platform work exactly as intended, we utilize tiny data files called cookies that settle locally on your processing hardware.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are simple alphanumeric text strings that an online server drops onto your computer system or smartphone storage profile when you transition into web portals. They serve as reliable digital checkpoints, ensuring your device communicates its layout preferences or system choices efficiently during active or subsequent loading operations.

2. How We Use Cookies

Bonus Tax Calculator operates on clean, straightforward programmatic frameworks. We deploy cookies for the following operational categories:

  • Functional / Preference Tracking: To retain selected items like your custom state settings or withholding radio methods over short periods so you do not have to reset them during form evaluations.
  • Analytics & Diagnostics: Anonymous platform tags monitor page performance speeds, conversion flows, and asset loads. This performance evaluation data is completely aggregate and anonymous.

3. Third-Party Analytic Integrations

In selective environments, we coordinate with trusted analytical diagnostic platforms to track structural web metrics. These partners may establish local web tags to catalog system speeds, referral paths, and layout engagement rates. This ensures we continue optimizing our asset loads for modern engines.

4. Managing and Overriding Cookie Status

You can prevent the setting of cookies by adjusting the settings on your browser (see your browser Help for how to do this). Be aware that disabling cookies will affect the functionality of this and many other websites that you visit. Disabling cookies will usually result in also disabling certain functionality and features of this site. Therefore, it is recommended that you do not disable cookies if they are used to provide a service that you use.

5. Updates and Compliance Changes

Our operational architecture might adapt occasionally due to code changes or shifting platform patterns. Any adjustments made to our tracking configuration profiles will immediately appear on this page.
